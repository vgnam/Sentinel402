# UniHackfest 2026: verified requirements and open questions

Checked on 27 September 2026 in the signed-in Corelia browser. The public web fetcher could not load these pages; the content below was read from their rendered pages. This is a preparation brief, not an eligibility determination.

## Eligibility and format

The program is free for university and college students in Vietnam, across disciplines. It is hybrid. Partner-school teams use their school qualifier; outside teams use the online Independent Teams bracket. A majority from one partner school determines the bracket. Teams select one primary track and one product theme. Team changes from the national round require organizer approval. Teams retain their IP and must represent their own work honestly. [Official overview](https://app.corelia.academy/hackathons/unihackfest-2026/overview)

## Tracks, themes, and ecosystem

| Primary track | Published emphasis |
|---|---|
| Best Product & Business | User need, working solution, business model, route to market |
| Best Technical Build | Technical depth, on/off-chain design, code quality, Solana |
| Best AI Product | Practical AI value for a real user problem |

Themes: RWA & Tokenization; Consumer dApps; DeFi & Digital Asset; AI x Web3. AI may support research, coding, and product features. Solana is the technical-track ecosystem; its learning path includes Rust, wallets, and frontend integration. EDU Chain / Open Campus verifies program credentials and badges; the pages do not say every project must deploy there. Technical demos use devnet/testnet, simulated data, or test wallets. [Overview](https://app.corelia.academy/hackathons/unihackfest-2026/overview)

The signed-in [project creation form](https://app.corelia.academy/projects/new?hackathon=unihackfest-2026), inspected without entering or saving anything, currently requires title, slug, summary, detailed description, and at least one track. Summary limit: 1,000 characters; description: 20,000; progress: 10,000. English or Vietnamese may be primary; translation is optional. Logo, up to six screenshots, demo URL, pitch deck, repository, demo video, pitch video, and progress are optional in the form. Resource links should be HTTPS and accessible to reviewers. Invited team members must be registered, accept their invitation, and then appear publicly. Projects are public. These are form requirements; they do not guarantee competitive adequacy or award eligibility.

## Official help and missing information

Resources link the Solana Vibe Coding course, Solana docs/devnet faucet/explorer, Rust Book, EDU Chain docs, idea pool, candidate groups, and organizer contact. The five-course path is incomplete and training videos are still being edited. [Official resources](https://app.corelia.academy/hackathons/unihackfest-2026/resources)

Resolve these with the organizers before submitting:

- **Track/theme mismatch:** overview says one primary track plus one theme; the form allows multiple track selections and shows no dedicated theme field. Use one primary track and state the theme in the description pending clarification.
- **Deliverable ambiguity:** overview says prepare product, demo, source, and team details; the form accepts ideas and makes all resource links optional. Provide working evidence anyway; optional form fields are not a waiver of track expectations.
- **Round 2 presentation scoring:** no demo is scheduled, but two rubrics still include presentation criteria. How videos/written materials substitute is unspecified.
- **Missing details:** team-size limits, detailed student verification, treatment of pre-existing work/AI assistance, exact video/pitch length, AI scoring weights, award tie-breaks, and voting rules are not established by the three pages.
- **Formal rule document:** the prizes page links a [Google document](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1gveC_I-KCVGpC9PP94D2TDx_vEP3TXbBHKY0dgvxw5M/edit). Its title loaded, but its full body could not be extracted in this review; additional rules may apply. Do not treat this brief as a full review of that document or later candidate-group announcements.
- **Team-specific eligibility:** school composition and member status are not known from the repository. Account registration alone does not resolve them.
